Routing

Shop pickup days

Best order plans: home → shop (on pickup days) → stops → home. Drive times add rush-hour slowdowns (7–9:30 AM, 3:30–6:30 PM). Once you finish a stop or tap “I'm here” on the map, it plans from there.

School

Class days (go straight from work)

On class days the plan ends at school instead of home and tells you when to leave your last stop. The drive to Toronto is counted 50% slower for 401 rush hour.

Smarts

Text to customers

{first} = customer's first name, {addr} = address, {min} = minutes away, {eta} = arrival time, {me} = your name. The text opens in Messages for you to send — nothing is sent by itself.

Piece rates

Day pay = hours × hourly + bonus. Bonus = piece total minus regular pay when piece is higher. Weeks run Sunday to Saturday. Amounts are before tax. Marking a stop done adds: REPL_MRT → REPL MRT install, REPL_T → TC outside, REPAIR → TP repair investigation.
